# Task 1: Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard created to analyze sales, profit, and product performance across different regions and sub-categories.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, Slicers, Dashboard Charts)

## Key Insights
- **Top Region:** West leads in overall sales and profit, followed by East.
- **Underperforming Regions:** Central and South show lower revenue and margin performance.
- **Top Category:** Technology generates the highest sales volume.

## Actionable Recommendations
- Reallocate marketing budget to high-ROI regions (West & East).
- Review pricing structure and supply chain costs for Central & South regions.
- Bundle or liquidate underperforming items (Fasteners, Envelopes).
